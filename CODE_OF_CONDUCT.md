# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep disagreements about the evidence. Arguing about sources and figures is welcome. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, report it privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/nickjlamb/ai-footprint/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [PharmaTools.AI](https://www.pharmatools.ai). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block contributors who repeatedly do.
